01 · The problem

Forests are vast.
Surveillance isn't.

India's forests span vast, often inaccessible terrain. Manual patrols, static CCTV, and occasional drone flights leave critical gaps — poaching, illegal logging, encroachment, and early-stage fires go undetected until it's too late.

01

Limited coverage

Vast, remote forest terrain is impossible to patrol continuously on foot.

02

Personnel shortage

Forest departments are chronically understaffed for the area they guard.

03

Delayed detection

Incidents are discovered hours or days after they occur.

04

Poor night visibility

Most poaching and logging happens after dark — unseen by daytime patrols.

05

No intelligent analysis

Raw footage with no automated threat detection or prioritization.

06

High patrol risk

Ground teams face direct physical danger confronting armed poachers.

Dual-tier compute — edge & cloud

The REST proxy in this demo is the same abstraction a Jetson container exposes on the drone itself.

Always available

Ground C2 (cloud)

Deep reasoning, on demand

04 · Innovation

Not an LLM wrapper. An autonomy stack.

Six design decisions that separate VanRakshak from a drone with a camera and a chatbot.

Conservative by design

Wildlife alerts and ranger dispatch — but never the human-intruder siren for elephants. Escalation is gated by persistent, confirmed evidence.

735 detections · 0 sirens for wildlife

Evidence before action

Every transition and command carries evidence references, threshold values, and deterministic reason codes.

AUDIT TRAIL, NOT A BLACK BOX

Deterministic FSM safety

Geofence breach and low-battery return-to-home are enforced by deterministic safety code — outside the model.

LLMs NEVER TOUCH MOTORS

Closed-loop actuator ACK

Commands declare intent; the hardware layer acknowledges execution. SENT → ACKNOWLEDGED in real time.

SIREN · SPOTLIGHT · GIMBAL · PAYLOAD

>98% bandwidth reduction

We ship event JSON and 20–40 KB keyframes — not video streams. That's what makes a multi-drone fleet on one ground station feasible.

SWARM-SCALE BY CONSTRUCTION

Offline fail-safe

If the VLM drops, spatial-persistence threat scoring takes over — flight and safety never depend on the cloud.

GRACEFUL DEGRADATION, TESTED

The threat score fuses four independent signals — each one explainable.

Threat = 0.35·VLM + 0.25·Detector + 0.25·ZoneRisk + 0.15·Acoustic
